Real physics, simplified for the classroom: seismic rays are traced through a layered Earth with Snell's law, the survey solves the acoustic wave equation, the mantle runs a Rayleigh–Bénard convection solver, and the storm uses the Shue magnetopause and Burton Dst models. Simulations are time-lapsed so a whole process fits in about a minute.
