Geophysics Sim Lab

Seven narrated simulations across the main branches of geophysics, from earthquake waves to the solar wind. Pick a branch, move the controls, and press Play lesson.

Voice: Karen

Narration uses the Karen voice, which comes with Mac, iPhone and iPad. On a Mac, Safari or Chrome both work.
